Arm 101, its simulated gripper, practice can, work surface, and all other scene
geometry are original primitive constructions. Arm 101 is a synthetic teaching
preset, not a vendor model or a digital twin of the physical Arm Alliance rig.

Named hardware presets use a small set of public specification values as factual reference data and are rendered with original primitive geometry:

- Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Standard: 4608 × 2592, nominal 66° horizontal × 41° vertical field of view.
- Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Wide: 4608 × 2592, nominal 102° horizontal × 67° vertical field of view.
- Luxonis OAK-D Lite color camera: nominal 69° horizontal × 54° vertical field of view.
- OpenMANIPULATOR-X: four arm degrees of freedom plus gripper, nominal 380 mm reach.
- Simplified six-axis cobot reference: six rotating joints and nominal 850 mm reach, based on the UR5e specification.

All camera rendering uses an ideal-pinhole approximation. The agent observation
is structured analytic projection data, not physical camera evidence. All named
robot geometry is a simplified, independently drawn kinematic reference rather
than vendor CAD. The OpenMANIPULATOR-X gripper specification is reference data;
it does not describe an installed gripper on the physical Arm Alliance rig.
